About This Website

My name is Anja and I have created this website to show what accessible design is, why accessibility is important, and how to create an accessible website. My goal in creating this website is to help inform you about the importance of accessibility in the design of online products. Around 46.3 million people living in the United States have a disability. This makes up almost 14 percent of the population. In this website you’ll learn about why it’s important to account for this percentage of our population, what types of disabilities online applications need to be able to help with, and so much more. Thank you for following along and enjoy this deep dive into everything online accessibility.
