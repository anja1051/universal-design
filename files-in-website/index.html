About Me

My name is Anja Delzell. I am a CS and Pre law student at the University of Colorado Boulder. I enjoy poetry, reading, yoga, rock climbing and spending time with my family and my awesome roomates. I also love to ski and am super excited for ski season. I am currently reading the book Assassin's Apprentice by Robin Hobb and have started Empire of the Vampire by Jay Kristoff. Other than reading and classwork I am applying for law schools I practice with my band, and I spend lots of time with my roomates and family.

Poem

Here is a short poem:

Magic:
You don't believe in magic,
But don't deny,
A world flows through us,
Keeping us alive.
And that miracle we call life,
That is magic.
